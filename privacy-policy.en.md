# Privacy Policy (Coin Pusher Voyage)

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how the developer of Coin Pusher Voyage (the "App") handles information about its users.

## 1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal information. The developer does not run any servers, and the App does not send any information to the developer.

Your game progress (coins, level, records, prizes, settings, etc.) is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## 2. Information sent to third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each service handles information under its own privacy policy.

### Google AdMob (advertising)

- Information: advertising ID, device information (model, OS version, language, etc.), IP address (used to estimate an approximate region), ad impressions and taps, and app performance information
- Purposes: serving ads, measuring ads, and preventing fraud; with your consent, showing ads based on your interests (personalized ads)
- Recipient: Google LLC (United States)
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data in ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Google Play Games Services (online rankings, only if you sign in)

- Information: your Play Games player ID and player name, and your stage clear times
- Purpose: showing online rankings
- Recipient: Google LLC (United States)
- Nothing is sent unless you sign in.

## 3. Consent and how to change it

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent management tool when they first open the App. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → About this app → Privacy settings.

All users can reset or delete their advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in their device settings (for example, Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; this varies by device).

## 4. International transfers

The information above may be processed by Google LLC on servers outside your country, including the United States.

## 5. Children

Because the App contains simulated gambling elements (coin pusher and slot machine), it is not directed at children under 13.

## 6. Money and prizes

Coins and prizes in the App cannot be exchanged for money or real-world goods. The App has no in-app purchases.
